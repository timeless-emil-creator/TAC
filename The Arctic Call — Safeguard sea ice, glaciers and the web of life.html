The Arctic is calling. Will you answer?

A global network of citizens and leaders answering a call from the Arctic, and a call to the world, to safeguard sea ice, glaciers and the web of life for future generations.

Join the network See the expedition

One mission: save the Arctic ice, protect the world’s future.

Sea ice and glaciers are our planet’s early-warning system. The Arctic is changing fast, and so is the cryosphere all over the world.

Combining wisdom, science and lived experience, we empower positive climate action by inspiring, educating and engaging people everywhere. Hope is active: when we act together, big dreams become reality.

Three ways we turn concern into action

Connect

Bring people to the living Arctic, so hope and action start from direct experience.

Inspire

Use stories, science and Indigenous wisdom to build a sense of global responsibility.

Act at home

Empower concrete steps in your own community to protect Earth’s frozen regions.

What the Arctic teaches us about our planet’s future

Our expeditions take a collaborative cohort of women leaders and supportive allies from many nations to Svalbard in the high Arctic: five days aboard the MS Serenissima, immersed in the ice, the wildlife and each other’s ideas.

Focus
Leadership, Indigenous knowledge, science storytelling and creative action.
Format
A ship-based Arctic journey with daily landings, citizen science and a 1.5-hour learning circle before dinner.
Outcomes
Personal leadership growth, community action plans, educator and media toolkits, and a lasting network.

Questions, answered

Can I contact a local representative in my own country or region?

Yes. Tell us where you’re from or which language you’re most comfortable in, and we’ll find someone to help you.

Will I see any wildlife?

You will experience Arctic wildlife. That may include walruses, seals, whales, puffins and other birds, Arctic foxes, reindeer and, with luck, polar bears.

Do I need to be physically fit to join?

No, but general good health is appropriate. If you have concerns about your physical abilities, please contact us.

Will it be cold?

It’s late summer, so expect around 5 °C with some wind chill. Good layering keeps you warm, and we send a clothing list after you sign up.
